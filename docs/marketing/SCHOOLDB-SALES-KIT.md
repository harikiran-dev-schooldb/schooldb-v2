# SchoolDB Sales Kit

> Internal sales and marketing guide for school demonstrations.

## Positioning

### SchoolDB
**One platform to run your school with confidence.**

SchoolDB brings core school operations into one structured web application so school teams can manage academic setup, students, teachers, attendance, examinations, fees, homework and operational data in a connected workflow.

## What to Demonstrate

1. **Dashboard** — Give management a quick operational view.
2. **Academic Year** — Establish the active academic structure.
3. **Classes & Sections** — Show how the school's academic organisation is represented.
4. **Students & Enrolments** — Search and manage student information in context.
5. **Teachers** — Show teacher records and organisation.
6. **Attendance** — Demonstrate day-to-day attendance workflows and visibility.
7. **Examinations** — Show exam setup and exam-specific workflows.
8. **Fees & Receipts** — Demonstrate fee tracking and receipt workflows.
9. **Homework** — Show academic task management.
10. **Bulk Operations** — Show how larger data operations can be handled efficiently.

## 3–5 Minute Demo Script

### Opening
“SchoolDB is designed to bring your school's day-to-day administrative operations into one connected system.”

### 1. Dashboard
“This gives school management a quick operational picture without having to move between multiple files and disconnected processes.”

### 2. Students
“Student information is centrally organised so staff can find the right student and work with that record in context.”

### 3. Academic Structure
“We first structure the academic environment with the academic year, classes and sections. The rest of the school workflow operates around that structure.”

### 4. Attendance
“Attendance becomes part of the same student and academic structure, giving the school a consistent place to manage and review attendance.”

### 5. Examinations
“Examination workflows are kept within the same school environment rather than being managed as a separate process.”

### 6. Fees
“Fees and receipts are managed as part of the same platform, giving administration a clearer view of collections and outstanding work.”

### Close
“Instead of managing separate systems for separate activities, SchoolDB gives your school one connected operating platform.”

**Closing question:**
“Would you like us to show this using your school's actual workflow?”

## Objection Handling

### “We already use Excel.”
“SchoolDB gives you a structured application around the same school information, so teams spend less time maintaining disconnected files and more time operating the school.”

### “Our staff are not very technical.”
“The demo focuses on everyday school workflows rather than technical complexity. We can walk your team through the exact processes they perform.”

### “Can you demonstrate our school's workflow?”
“Yes. A personalised demonstration is the strongest next step. We can map the demo around your academic structure, student operations and administrative processes.”

### “Why should we change our current system?”
“The right comparison is not just feature count. It is how easily your staff can run everyday school operations in one consistent environment.”

## Sales Rules

- Lead with school problems and outcomes, not technology.
- Use a populated demo school whenever possible.
- Show the product instead of describing every feature.
- Never promise a feature that has not been verified in the current product.
- Finish every demo with a specific next step: personalised demo, pilot discussion, or onboarding conversation.

## Suggested Sales Flow

**Lead → Brochure → Live Demo → Personalised Demo → Pilot / Onboarding**

## Important Claim Discipline

Use only claims that can be demonstrated in the current SchoolDB release. Do not claim payment gateways, parent mobile apps, SMS/WhatsApp automation, transport management, payroll, library management, biometric integration, or other roadmap items unless they are implemented and verified in the product.

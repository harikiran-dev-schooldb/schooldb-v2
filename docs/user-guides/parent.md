# Parent User Guide

The Parent account opens **Student space** for every linked child. It is designed for viewing school information and completing permitted parent actions.

## Choose a child

If more than one student is linked to the account, SchoolDB first shows **Choose a student**. Select a child to open that student's dashboard. Use **Switch student** in the header to move between children.

If a child is missing or incorrectly linked, contact the school. Do not create another login as a workaround.

## Student dashboard

The dashboard summarizes attendance, active homework, fee balance, the next exam, and upcoming school events. Available modules include:

- Attendance
- Fees and installments
- Homework
- Timetable
- Exams and results
- Report card
- Leave and permission requests
- Transport
- Library
- Secure documents
- Calendar and notifications

## Pay fees

1. Open the child's **Fees** page.
2. Review the fee assignment, installment, amount, and outstanding balance.
3. Select the payable items and continue to the hosted payment page.
4. Complete payment once.
5. Return to SchoolDB and verify the status/receipt.

If the result is pending or unclear, do not immediately pay again. Wait for confirmation and contact the school Accountant with the payment reference.

## Submit leave or permission

Open **Leave**, choose the request type, enter dates/times and reason, then submit. Track the status from the same page. Leave requests may be queued offline and submitted automatically when the device reconnects.

## Notifications and settings

Use the bell for school updates and the calendar icon for events. In **Settings**, review the account and open PWA/device options. The Parent account cannot change official student records; request corrections from the school.

## Privacy

Use a personal device where possible. Do not share screenshots containing results, fee records, documents, transport details, or other children's information.

# Accountant User Guide

The Accountant's default access covers the dashboard, student lookup, fees and accounts, staff/payroll, inventory, and support queries. An administrator may adjust these permissions.

## Daily collection workflow

1. Open **Fees → Collect Fees**.
2. Find the student by admission number or name and verify the class/section.
3. Review assigned installments, prior payments, concessions/adjustments, and outstanding balance.
4. Select the correct items and payment method.
5. Confirm amount, reference, and date before saving.
6. Issue or print the generated receipt.
7. Check **Payment History** to confirm the transaction appears once.

Never record a second payment because a screen response was slow. First check Payment History, Receipts, and UPI Verification.

## UPI and online payments

- Use **UPI Verification** for payments requiring review.
- Match the payer reference, amount, student, and provider status.
- Treat a provider-accepted status and a completed school ledger entry as separate checks.
- Do not mark a payment successful without the evidence required by school policy.

Online payment and live verification require internet access.

## Fee setup and outstanding balances

- **Fee Categories** define charge types.
- **Fee Plans** define the amount/installment structure.
- Assign plans to the correct student group and academic year.
- Use **Outstanding Fees** for follow-up, but verify filters and student status before contacting a family.
- Preserve historical payments when a student becomes inactive; do not delete the financial trail.

## Expenses, payroll, and inventory

- Record expenses with the correct category, date, amount, payee, and reference.
- Void or reverse errors according to policy instead of hiding the original transaction.
- Use **Staff Attendance & Payroll** for attendance, payroll entries, and payslips.
- Use **Inventory & Assets** for stock and asset movement; document adjustments.

## Reconciliation checklist

- Compare cash, bank/UPI, and online totals with SchoolDB Payment History.
- Review duplicates, failed or pending payments, and unverified UPI entries.
- Confirm receipt sequence and voided transactions.
- Reconcile expenses and payroll totals.
- Export only the required date range and keep the file secure.
- Escalate unexplained differences before closing the period.

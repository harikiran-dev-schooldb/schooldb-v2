# Principal / School Admin User Guide

The Principal or School Admin manages the complete operation of one school. In SchoolDB, Principal and Vice Principal accounts use the protected `SCHOOL_ADMIN` role with their designation shown separately.

## Daily start

1. Review **Dashboard** for current totals and items requiring attention.
2. Check **Attendance Overview**, low attendance, and incomplete sessions.
3. Review fee collections/outstanding balances and operational alerts.
4. Check parent queries, leave requests, and notifications.
5. Use **Management Analytics** for trends and **Reports** for detailed exports.

## Academic setup

Use **Academic Setup** to maintain academic years, syllabi/branches, classes, sections, subjects, class subjects, teacher allocations, and class teachers. Build this foundation before attendance, timetable, exams, or fee assignments.

Use **Timetable → School Periods** before building the timetable. Review Daily, Class, and Teacher views after publishing changes.

## People and access

- **Students** — create and maintain student profiles. Inactivate a student who has left when records must be retained.
- **Teachers** — create teachers and their linked login.
- **Enrollments** — place active students in the correct academic year, class, and section.
- **Online Admissions** — review applications and convert approved applicants carefully.
- **User Accounts** — create Accountant and Receptionist accounts, activate/deactivate staff, and delegate custom module permissions.

Only the Super Admin can create or change administrator-level accounts. A School Admin can delegate permissions to non-admin staff but cannot change their own permissions.

## Core operations

- **Attendance** — monitor daily completion, reports, rankings, and low-attendance cases. Unlock or correct a locked session only under school policy.
- **Learning** — oversee homework, exam schedules, marks, results, report cards, and toppers.
- **Fees** — manage plans, categories, assignments, collection, receipts, payments, verification, outstanding balances, and expenses.
- **School Operations** — manage payroll, visitors, health, assets, pickup, maintenance, library, transport, calendar, and leave.
- **Communication** — publish notifications, resolve queries, manage parent queries, and review supported WhatsApp activity.

## Reports and review

Use operational pages for today's work, **Reports** for formal filtered outputs, and **Management Analytics** for leadership trends. Apply the correct academic year and date range before drawing conclusions. Inactive students should not appear in active operational lists, while historical records may remain available where needed for audit and reporting.

## Recommended monthly control checks

- Reconcile payment totals, receipts, UPI verification, expenses, and outstanding balances.
- Review attendance completion and low-attendance interventions.
- Verify enrollment counts against active students.
- Review staff permissions and deactivate unused accounts.
- Review audit logs, unresolved queries, maintenance tickets, inventory exceptions, and overdue library items.
- Export only approved reports and store them securely.

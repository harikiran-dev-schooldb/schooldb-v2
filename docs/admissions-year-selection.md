Admissions availability is independent of the single active teaching year. New academic years are available for internal admissions by default, without activating teaching. Migration enables existing active and later years per school, preserving historical data. Admins can close/reopen individual admission years, and closing disables the separate public enquiry portal. Applications, enquiry/follow-up lists, analytics, calculator, fee and seat configuration default to latest enabled year; all queries remain school-scoped. Existing full public application form retains active teaching-year behavior; public enquiry portal for future years requires explicit admin enablement. No teaching-year changes or legacy record import.

Admissions availability is independent of the single active teaching year. New academic years are available for internal admissions by default, without activating teaching. Migration enables existing active and later years per school, preserving historical data. Admins can close or reopen individual admission years, and closing disables the separate public enquiry portal. Applications, enquiry and follow-up lists, analytics, calculator, fee, and seat configuration default to the latest enabled year; all queries remain school-scoped. The full public application form remains limited to the active teaching year and now also requires that year to be enabled for admissions. The public enquiry portal is the explicit path for future years and requires separate administrator enablement. No teaching-year changes or legacy record import are implied.

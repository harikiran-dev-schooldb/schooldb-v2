# Admission workspace
New dedicated age calculator, enquiry age/fee preview, staff fee quotes, admin capacity, analytics, settings, public enquiry inbox, closed enquiry recovery, and sidebar links. Age rules stay unconfigured until school approval; no hardcoded school policy. Exact age uses completed UTC calendar months and residual days, consistent with legacy app. Fee quotes are informational, not ERP fee plans, and no fee assignments or payments are created. Capacity is a planning target. Public enquiry consent and rate limit are server enforced; portal is disabled by default and needs school approval and additional bot protection assessment before broad publication. Dismissal redacts personal details; recovery only reopens closed enquiries and never restores deleted records. Legacy import, fee mapping to ERP fee plans, push reminders, full end-to-end isolation testing and deleted application recovery remain separate. No legacy source data is transferred. Validate CI before merge.
